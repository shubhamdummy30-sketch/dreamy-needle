The Studio & Artisan

Slow craft, pure cotton floss, & the beauty of handmade stitches.

Dreamy Needle was founded by Shruti with one simple belief: in an era of instant fast fashion and computerized factory embroidery, there is an irreplaceable beauty in taking the time to sew by hand.

Every piece you see in our collection begins as a blank piece of fabric, a wooden hoop, and an idea. From tiny initial monograms on collar tips to elaborate botanical wedding date displays, each stitch is formed with human patience and care.

Our Philosophy

Why Hand Embroidery Matters

Why we choose slow hand stitching over industrial machines.

precision_manufacturing Machine & Factory Work

Flat, Rigid & Mass Produced

  • close Uses computerized vector scripts that feel stiff, uniform, and devoid of tactile nuance.
  • close Heavy scratchy backing paper often left inside garments, irritating skin on tees.
  • close Hundreds of identical copies made in seconds with synthetic polyester threads.
Dreamy Needle Standard
back_hand 100% Hand Needlework

Tactile, Organic & One-of-a-Kind

  • check_circle Varied tension and relief: 3D French knots, satin floral fills, and delicate backstitch outlines that catch light softly.
  • check_circle Skin-friendly finish: stabilizer is dissolved or carefully trimmed, leaving only soft cotton thread against your skin.
  • check_circle Truly personal: each piece takes hours of focused craft and carries meaningful intention.

Colorfast Cotton Floss

We use high-grade 6-strand cotton embroidery floss from leading Indian and European mills. The dyes are colorfast and resist bleeding, ensuring your personalized clothes remain vibrant wash after wash.

yard

Natural Woven Fabrics

From 220 GSM bio-washed combed cotton jersey to crisp breathable European linen and unbleached cotton calico for hoops, we pair thread tension carefully to ensure clean stitches without puckering.

Heirloom Unboxing

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about custom orders, care, and delivery.

Small monograms and handkerchiefs take 2-4 stitching days. T-shirts and shirts take 3-5 days. Detailed hoop art, date frames, and kurti yokes require 5-8 days. We recommend gentle hand-washing in cool water with mild liquid detergent. If machine washing, turn the garment inside out and place it inside a mesh laundry bag on a gentle cycle. Always iron on the reverse side of the embroidery or place a thin cotton pressing cloth over the stitches to maintain their 3D texture.

All Dreamy Needle creations use premium colorfast cotton stranded floss. For lasting beauty, wash in cold water with mild detergent and iron on the reverse side.
